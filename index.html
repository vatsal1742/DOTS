How to Play

1. On your turn, tap between two neighbouring dots to draw a line.

2. Close the 4th side of a box to win it — your initial goes inside and you score a point.

3. With “Extra turn” on, you play again after closing a box.

4. When every box is taken, the most boxes wins.

Online: one person taps Create Room and shares the 5-letter code; friends enter it under Join a Friend. iPhone and Android can play together.

Tip: switching apps is fine — the game is saved online, and you reconnect automatically when you come back.
