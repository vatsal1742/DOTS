How to Play

1. You start at the van 🚐 outside a dark house. One ghost lives in one room. Walk with the left side of the screen, look with the right side.

2. Use your tools to find 3 evidences: 📟 EMF level 5 · 📻 Spirit Box answers (flashlight OFF) · 🥶 temperature below 0 °C · 🖐️ UV hand prints · 📖 writing in a book · 🔮 orbs on the night-vision camera · ✨ figure in the dots projector.

3. Mark evidence in the 📓 journal — it filters the 12 ghost types. Then go back to the van and Submit.

4. The dark drains your 🧠 sanity. When the team's sanity is low the ghost hunts: lights flicker, the screen turns red. Run, break line of sight or hide in a closet. Crucifixes stop hunts, incense stops a hunt in progress, pills restore sanity.

Online: up to 4 friends hunt together — share findings in chat! Desktop: WASD + arrows, Space = use, F = light, Q/E = tool, J = journal.

1. Players take turns drawing. The drawer picks one of 3 secret words (easy, medium, hard).

2. Draw it — no letters or numbers! Use colours, brush sizes, eraser, undo and clear.

3. Everyone else guesses. Faster guesses score more (up to 550), and the drawer gets 60 for each player who guesses.

4. Letters are revealed as hints when time runs low. After all rounds, the most points wins.

Pass & Play: one phone — shout your guesses and tap who got it. Online: type guesses on your own phone and watch the drawing live.

1. Players take turns firing. Set the angle and power with the buttons — or just drag on the battlefield to aim.

2. Shells follow real physics: gravity pulls them down and the wind (top right) pushes them sideways.

3. Explosions blow holes in the ground. Tanks slide down when the dirt under them disappears.

4. Damage you deal = points for you. Hitting yourself costs points. Each weapon can be used once.

5. Drive left/right with ◀ ▶ (4 moves per match). After everyone has fired all shots, the most points wins.

Weapon Lab: design your own cannon balls — shells, spread, blast, gravity, bounces, splitting, rolling, digging, napalm or dirt!

1. Two players take turns placing ✕ and ◯ on a 3 × 3 grid.

2. Get three of your marks in a row — across, down or diagonally — to win.

3. If all 9 squares fill up with no winner, it's a draw. The starter swaps every game, and the series score keeps count.

1. Everyone secretly picks Stone ✊, Paper ✋ or Scissors ✌️. Picks are revealed together.

2. Stone breaks Scissors, Scissors cut Paper, Paper covers Stone. Winners score 1 point.

3. With 3 or more players: if all three signs (or only one sign) appear, the round is a draw.

4. First to the target score wins the match.

1. Everyone fills a 5 × 5 board with the numbers 1–25 in any order (or taps Shuffle). Keep it secret!

2. Players take turns calling one number from their own board. Everyone crosses that number off.

3. Every full row, column or diagonal lights one letter of B-I-N-G-O.

4. The first player to complete 5 lines wins. If two players get there on the same call, it's a tie.

Online: create a room, share the code, then everyone arranges their board and taps Ready.

Chat: in online rooms, tap the blue 💬 button to chat with everyone.

1. On your turn, tap between two neighbouring dots to draw a line.

2. Close the 4th side of a box to win it — your initial goes inside and you score a point.

3. With “Extra turn” on, you play again after closing a box.

4. When every box is taken, the most boxes wins.

Online: one person taps Create Room and shares the 5-letter code; friends enter it under Join a Friend. iPhone and Android can play together.

Tip: switching apps is fine — the game is saved online, and you reconnect automatically when you come back.
